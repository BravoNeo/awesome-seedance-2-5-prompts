# Content rights

This independent ReelDance catalog credits the creators who shared the original prompts and videos. Each entry links to the original prompt post and the video post on X. Model attribution comes from what the creator disclosed about the clip.

The editable prompts are ReelDance adaptations. Creator videos and original posts stay on X. Follow the creator’s terms before reusing their text, footage, artwork or reference assets.

Creators can open a repository issue to request a correction or removal. Include the catalog entry and original post URL.
